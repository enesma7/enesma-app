How To Check

  
    
    
  1. Open the signed PDF in Adobe Acrobat or another PDF reader that shows digital signatures.
    2. 
    
  2. Confirm the signature field is named ENESMAEsignSeal and uses an invisible PKCS#7 detached signature. The document pages should look unchanged.
    3. 
    
  3. The signature should validate as intact. Because this certificate is self-signed (not issued by a public CA or AATL), the reader will report that the issuer is unknown or that the certificate is untrusted. That warning is expected.
    4. 
    
  4. Compare the certificate’s SHA-256 fingerprint with the fingerprint on this page and on the Certificate of Completion footer.
    5. 
    
  5. If even one byte of the signed file is changed, the signature check must fail (digest mismatch).
