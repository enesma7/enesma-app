ENESMA E-Sign Privacy Policy

ENESMA E-Sign is an internal document-signing tool operated by ENESMA Realty for its agents and admins.

When a user connects Google, the app requests only their email address and permission to send email (gmail.send). This is used solely to send signing invitations, reminders and completion notices from the user's own address. The app does not read, store, or delete any Gmail messages.

Documents, signer names, emails and signing records are stored in ENESMA Realty's Google Workspace and are not sold or shared with third parties. Users can disconnect Google at any time from their Profile or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
